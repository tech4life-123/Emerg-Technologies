# Competitor research (static-content review)

Inferences come from reading public pages, not from performance measurements. Reviewed October 2026.

| Site | Strengths worth learning from | Weaknesses to avoid |
|---|---|---|
| MTN / Lonestar Cell (lonestarcell.com) | Mega-menu, clear deal cards, trust-focused footer | Repeated blocks, placeholder `#` links, empty image alt text, typos, unclear mobile menu |
| Andela | Audience-split navigation, consistent "Book a discovery call" CTA, third-party rating and testimonials as trust signals | Heavy reliance on social proof Emerg cannot honestly claim yet |
| Flutterwave | Products organised by audience, global-reach section | Dense navigation |
| Vercel | Minimal dark system, theme handling, skip link | Little warmth or regional identity |

## Not reviewed
Orange Liberia and LTC could not be fetched (blocked by robots rules). The HeartBridge link could not be fetched either, so it is marked unverified on the site. A search for Liberia consultancy firms returned no usable firm names.

## Decisions taken
Audience-first navigation, one consistent CTA, a real skip link and alt text, honest status labels instead of invented metrics, no fabricated clients, awards or testimonials.
